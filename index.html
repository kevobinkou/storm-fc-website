The Story So Far

Since 2004

Two decades of matchdays, training sessions on borrowed pitches, and young players from Gitugi finding their footing. This timeline is a living record — replace the entries below with the club's real seasons and milestones.

2004

The club is founded

STORM FC is formed in Gitugi Ward under Muchoki's leadership, as a home for local football talent. Add the founding story here — who started it, and why.

— to —

A ward power, most seasons

STORM FC has won the Gitugi Ward league almost every year, and has sometimes carried that form all the way to the Mathioya Constituency level — regularly meeting rivals like Chui FC, Gikoe FC, and Youngstar FC in ward derbies and playoff finals.

2025

Gitugi Ward Champions

STORM FC are crowned Gitugi Ward Champions in the Murang'a County League — another title in a long run of ward-level success.

2026

Still going strong

Today, STORM FC continues developing young players under the same leadership that started it all in 2004, competing in local tournaments like Play to Prosperity and ward semis and finals at Gitugi Grounds.
